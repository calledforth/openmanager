What changed, and why

  1. No Inbox. It listed needs-you, done-unseen and failed; the home now carries all three without a second place: those sessions sit first in All, as their own groups (Needs you, Working, Done, Failed), with the marks and the done/failed washes the rows already had. The order is pinned → needs you → working → done and failed (news since you looked) → the rest, newest first within each.
  2. Top row is one row. A smaller title and the two views on the same 44px line; the environment line is gone. One environment is context, not news. With two, every row says where it runs (frame B) and nothing at the top has to.
  3. All · Settled, no days. All is what is unsettled; Settled is the long tail, flat and newest first. "Today / Yesterday / Tuesday" labels are gone; the age at the right of each row says when.
  4. Drafts are cards at the top of All, in the draft fill with the pen mark, as the web sidebar's Drafts section above Active. Swipe left to discard, as before. No Drafts tab.
  5. The dock replaces the pill. With Inbox gone a two-item pill says nothing, so the bottom becomes the actions you reach for: New chat as a wide glass pill in the middle (the primary thing, under the thumb), search at the right as before, and Settings at the left (the only other top-level place; rare, but a header button would have been the one thing left at the top for no reason). Settings is a page pushed from the home, with Back, not a tab.
  6. Project marks. A project with no declared icon shows the folder (web's FolderSimple), never a coloured tile; a declared icon shows as before (salesmaxai in the frames).

Open

  • Group labels in All cost ~28px each. They could go, leaving marks and order alone; kept for now because the owner kept the Active tab's grouping and Pinned's label is part of how pinned reads.
  • The environment label sits last on the row's second line. The alternative is a faint line under the age at the right; last-on-the-line was chosen so it is the first thing to truncate when a title's project and status need the room.
